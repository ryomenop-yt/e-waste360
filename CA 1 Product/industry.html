How the IT & Electronics Industry Creates E-Waste

The IT and electronics sector is one of the largest generators of e-waste. Let's explore the key drivers behind this growth.

Rapid Technology Upgrades

New phones, laptops and electronic products are released frequently, encouraging consumers to buy the latest versions.

Short Product Lifecycles

Some devices are replaced even when they are still usable, because they are seen as outdated or unsupported.

Electronic Component Waste

Manufacturing and repair activities can create discarded components, circuit boards and sub-assemblies.

Batteries & Accessories

Batteries, chargers, cables and other accessories also contribute significantly to electronic waste.

Consumer Replacement

Consumers may replace fully functioning products with newer models before the old ones wear out.

Planned & Software Obsolescence

Devices lose support or updates over time, pushing even functional hardware toward early replacement.
