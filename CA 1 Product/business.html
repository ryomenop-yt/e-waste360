How Businesses Can Reduce E-Waste

The IT and electronics industry can play a leading role in reducing e-waste through design, operations and responsibility.

Designing Longer-Lasting Products

Building durability and quality into products from the start.

Repairability

Designing products that are easy to open, fix and service.

Take-Back Programs

Offering programs where old devices are returned for reuse or recycling.

Responsible Recycling

Partnering with certified recyclers that meet environmental standards.

Reuse of Components

Recovering and reusing working parts rather than discarding them.

Better Inventory Management

Reducing overstock and obsolete stock that becomes e-waste.

Sustainable Packaging

Using recyclable, minimal and eco-friendly packaging materials.

Employee Awareness

Educating staff about responsible use, repair and disposal.

Responsible Disposal

Ensuring end-of-life equipment is handled safely and legally.

Circular Economy Practices

Keeping materials in use through design for recycling and recovery.

Linear vs Circular Model

Linear Model

Take→ Make→ Use→ Throw

Resources are extracted, used once and discarded — creating waste.

Circular Model

Design→ Use→ Repair→ Reuse→ Recover→ Recycle→ Remanufacture

Materials are kept in circulation for as long as possible, reducing waste.
