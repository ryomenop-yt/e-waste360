Journey

The E-Waste Lifecycle

From raw materials to responsible disposal — explore every stage of an electronic product's journey. Click each stage for details.

Raw Materials

Metals, plastics and rare earths are mined and processed.

Manufacturing

Components are built and assembled into finished products.

Distribution

Devices reach stores and consumers through supply chains.

Consumer Use

The product is used daily by consumers or businesses.

Repair / Upgrade

Devices are fixed or upgraded to extend their useful life.

Reuse

Working devices are donated, resold or repurposed.

Collection

Retired electronics are gathered through collection points.

Recycling

Devices are processed to reclaim materials and components.

Material Recovery

Precious metals and reusable materials are extracted.

Safe Disposal

Hazardous and non-recoverable residues are handled safely.
