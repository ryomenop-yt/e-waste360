What Can YOU Do?

Everyone has a role to play in reducing e-waste. Pick the group that matches you.

  • Use electronics for longer
  • Repair devices instead of replacing
  • Donate usable devices
  • Recycle batteries responsibly
  • Avoid unnecessary upgrades
  • Buy durable products
  • Check repair options first
  • Use take-back programs
  • Recycle responsibly at authorised centres
  • Establish e-waste collection
  • Promote repair and reuse
  • Work with responsible recyclers
  • Reduce electronic waste during operations
