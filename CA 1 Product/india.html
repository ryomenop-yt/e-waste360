E-Waste Management in India

India is one of the largest generators of e-waste. Managing it responsibly requires the coordinated effort of many stakeholders.

Government

Sets regulations, authorises recyclers, and works to build formal collection and recycling infrastructure.

Electronics Manufacturers

Design products and manage end-of-life obligations, including Extended Producer Responsibility.

Consumers

Buy, use and dispose of devices responsibly, and return e-waste to authorised channels.

Collection Centres

Receive and consolidate e-waste from households and businesses for onward processing.

Recyclers

Dismantle, segregate and recover materials, and safely dispose of hazardous residues.

Businesses

Manage their own electronic assets, follow e-waste rules and support responsible disposal of IT equipment.

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)

EPR means that the companies that produce or import electronics are responsible for what happens to their products at the end of their life. Instead of leaving e-waste to governments or consumers alone, producers must organise collection and recycling for the devices they sell.

In simple terms: “If you make or sell the product, you are responsible for managing its waste.” This encourages producers to design longer-lasting, easier-to-repair and more recyclable products, while financing collection and recycling systems.
