Four Principles, One Mission

Every electronic device deserves a responsible life cycle

Reuse

Give electronic devices a second life instead of discarding them. Donate, resell or repurpose working electronics.

Repair

Fix devices that can be repaired rather than instantly replacing them. A small repair can add years of life.

Recycle

Send unusable electronics to responsible recycling channels so valuable materials can be recovered.

Reduce

Reduce unnecessary electronic consumption at the source. Buy only what you truly need.
