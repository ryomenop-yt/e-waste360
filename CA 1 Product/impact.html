Environmental Impact of E-Waste

When e-waste is dumped or disposed of improperly, it can create serious environmental problems. Click each card to expand it.

🌍 Soil Pollution

Heavy metals and toxins seep into the ground.

Lead, mercury and cadmium leak from dumped electronics into soil, poisoning land and making it unsafe for years.

💧 Water Pollution

Chemicals enter rivers and groundwater.

Toxic substances from e-waste can contaminate water sources, harming aquatic life and human communities downstream.

🌫️ Air Pollution

Burning e-waste releases toxic fumes.

Informal burning of cables and boards releases dangerous gases like dioxins, harming air quality and health.

⚠️ Hazardous Substances

Devices contain dangerous materials.

Batteries, screens and circuit boards contain hazardous substances that require careful, certified handling.

⛏️ Resource Loss

Valuable materials are lost forever.

Discarding e-waste wastes recoverable gold, copper and rare earths, forcing more mining and extraction.
