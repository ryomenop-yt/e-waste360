Process

The E-Waste Management Process

A step-by-step look at how e-waste is responsibly managed, from collection to safe disposal.

1

Collection

Electronic waste is collected separately through drop-off points, take-back programs and collection drives.

2

Segregation

Batteries, screens, circuit boards and plastic casings are grouped for suitable processing.

3

Data Protection

Devices that store personal data are securely wiped or destroyed before recycling.

4

Dismantling

Devices are manually or mechanically separated into individual components and materials.

5

Material Recovery

Useful materials like gold, copper, aluminum and plastics are recovered for further processing.

6

Recycling

Recoverable materials are processed and refined so they can be used again in new products.

7

Safe Disposal

Non-recoverable hazardous materials are handled appropriately at certified facilities.

Action Framework

The 5Rs of Responsible Electronics

Five principles to keep electronics out of landfill and in circulation. Click each card to flip it.

R

REFUSE

Unnecessary purchases are waste waiting to happen

Refuse

Avoid unnecessary purchases. Say no to gadgets you don't truly need and resist upgrade pressure.

R

REDUCE

Less consumption means less waste

Reduce

Reduce unnecessary electronic consumption and buy only what you actually need.

R

REPAIR

A small fix can add years of life

Repair

Repair devices instead of immediately replacing them. A small fix can extend a device's life by years.

R

REUSE

Pass it on, don't throw it out

Reuse

Donate, resell or reuse working devices so they keep serving people instead of becoming waste.

R

RECYCLE

Recover materials responsibly

Recycle

Send unusable electronics through appropriate recycling channels so materials can be recovered.
